Services

After your photo session, you'll receive a password-protected link to your photos. Each photo is digitally enhanced, and I may do some light retouching if necessary. You will not need to pay extra for your photos, what you receive is all yours.

The prices below reflect the standard options described, but I'm flexible based on your needs! I may charge more for these extras, like processing the images faster than usual to meet a deadline, or adding an additional location. My standard turnaround time for images is 2 - 3 weeks.

Headshots

Need an image for your socials? Or maybe something for casting or promotional materials? This is the option for you! When you're looking for headshots, it's usually because you're at the precipace of something new. It's my job to make sure you're starting off with your best foot foward, with pictures that make you feel confident to tackle any task.

Service includes:

  • $75
  • 30-45 minutes
  • 1 location, 1 outfit
  • 5-10 digitally enhanced photos

Portraits

For photos that are more involved or artsy! If you've got a few different ideas you want to try out, or know what you want is a bit more involved than a headshot, I can help you execute your vision. Examples include couple's photos, pet portraits, artist materials, and more.

Service includes:

  • $125
  • 45 - 90 minutes
  • 1 location
  • 15-30 digitally enhanced photos
